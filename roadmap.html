Development atlas // Evidence before claims

Five paths.
One open science mission.

Follow the survey, the engines and the tools taking shape around it. Each track shows what is being built, what still needs scientific proof, and the evidence behind the next step.

Implementation and scientific validation are separate. Working software, converged calculations and completed tickets do not establish an independently validated scientific result.

No overall completion percentage is claimed. Milestones advance through evidence and review.

Status legend & evidence rules
Planned / Discovery
Scope or investigation; no completed implementation implied.
In development / In review
Implementation exists or is under review. Review-branch evidence is explicitly labeled.
Scientific HOLD / Not assessed
Required scientific proof is still owed or has not been evaluated.
Independently validated / Released
Reserved for linked independent scientific sign-off or an explicit release record.

Evidence dates identify this snapshot’s inspection date. They are not promised completion dates. Linear links describe scope; merged artifacts and reviewed scientific evidence carry separate labels.
