Read the distinctions

What a coverage record can tell you

Available
The named resource is held, or published but not acquired. That says nothing by itself about validation or reuse rights.
Unverified
Independent scientific assessment is still owed, even when the source identity and stored artifact are known.
Validated
Requires explicit independent sign-off for a stated scope. The current registry makes no such promotion.
Known gap
A documented incompatibility or missing requirement for this particular resource.
Not yet audited
The release, membership or physical domain has not been established. Missing from this registry does not mean nonexistent.
Outside scope
A requested value lies outside a stored axis envelope. Values inside an envelope still need exact line and hull checks.

Atom ≠ atmosphere ≠ solver

A model atom supplies levels and rates. An atmosphere supplies physical structure. A solver handles radiation and populations. A correction grid or surrogate summarizes a source calculation; a line list supplies transitions. None substitutes for the others.

Geometry and dimensionality

Disk-centre intensity and disk-integrated flux are different observables. Mean-3D atmospheres, independent 1.5D columns and coupled full-3D transfer are distinct. A solver’s full-3D capability does not validate every species, atom or diagnostic.

Source membership is not a grade

A transition may belong to several published selections. Recommendation, empirical suitability, experimental oscillator-strength quality and theoretical-calculation validation remain independent. Memberships and their reasons appear only when supplied by reviewed canonical evidence; RYA-1376 owns the pending source audit.

Wavelengths and uncertainty

Display bands use UV <400 nm, VIS 400–780 nm and IR ≥780 nm. These are navigation categories. Exact transitions, air/vacuum conventions, holding-specific telluric checks and uncertainty evidence determine scientific use. Missing chemistry, rates or grades remain explicit.
