    Start here

    A spectrum separates light by wavelength. Ultraviolet photons carry more energy than visible photons; infrared photons carry less. Wavelength is a length, frequency counts oscillations per second, and photon energy is the energy exchanged in one interaction.

    Go deeper · the mathematics

    In vacuum, c = λν and E = hν = hc/λ. At small radial velocities, Δλ/λ ≈ v/c; positive recession shifts a line to longer wavelength. Resolving power R = λ/Δλ describes the smallest nearby features an instrument can separate under a stated resolution convention.

    Advanced · assumptions and scientific limits

    Air and vacuum wavelengths differ because light propagates differently in air. Compare quantities in a declared medium and velocity frame. Instrumental resolution, pixel sampling and measurement precision are separate. UV, visible and IR band boundaries are conventions; this site uses <400, 400–780 and ≥780 nm for navigation.

    Worked example / activity

    A hypothetical 500 nm vacuum photon has frequency about 6.00 × 10¹⁴ Hz and energy 2.48 eV. At R = 50,000, a 500 nm resolution element is about 0.010 nm. These are unit-conversion examples, not observations.

    Common misconception

    A redshift moves line positions; it does not identify an element by itself. Greater resolution does not automatically mean smaller abundance uncertainty.

    Definitions: band · spectral resolution · barycentric correction

    Reference data, assumptions and teacher notes

    vacuum Ritz estimate from rounded NIST level differences; individual fine-structure components, not blended line centroids. No uncertainty inferred from printed decimals. Educational display rounded to 0.01 nm and 0.001 eV; not for precision calibration.

    • Schematic level spacing, electron marker and photon path are not spatial orbit trajectories or physical timescales.

    Classroom: predict the direction, play once, then reverse the mode. Does changing direction change the photon energy? Why does Balmer β have a shorter wavelength? Use the CSV to record the level identity and source.

    Stellar absorption is a radiative-transfer phenomenon involving opacity, emission, scattering and populations in an atmosphere; this isolated transition does not simulate a star.

    Sources: NIST hydrogen levels, NIST line-component context, SI constants. Versioned hydrogen reference data · Sodium levels and canonical metadata snapshot · NIST Na I levels. The sodium D doublet connects the same lower state to two fine-structure upper states. NIST Handbook sodium line wavelengths are air values; this explorer derives vacuum Ritz estimates from the energies.

    Start here

    An atom has discrete quantum states. Absorption can raise an electron to a higher-energy state; emission accompanies a drop to a lower-energy state. The photon energy matches the difference. The explorer below connects these two descriptions without depicting electrons as tiny planets.

    Go deeper · the mathematics

    Eγ = Eupper − Elower. When level energies are tabulated as wavenumbers in cm⁻¹, λvac(nm) = 10⁷ / Δσ. The same energy gap applies in either direction. Oscillator strength f describes a transition probability scale; it is not an elemental abundance.

    Advanced · assumptions and scientific limits

    Fine structure splits a named hydrogen feature into components. Electric-dipole selection rules restrict which states connect; not every pair of levels is allowed. Natural, thermal, pressure and turbulent broadening shape lines, while nearby transitions can blend. This explorer selects individual allowed components and does not calculate their intensities.

    Worked example / activity

    Select Balmer α and compare emission with absorption. The photon energy and wavelength stay the same while the direction reverses. Then choose Balmer β: its larger level gap requires a higher-energy, shorter-wavelength photon.

    Common misconception

    A stellar absorption line is not explained by one atom simply removing a color. Emergent intensity depends on opacity, emission, scattering, populations and the temperature structure along the path.

    Definitions: absorption line · oscillator strength · blend · forbidden line

    Model, sources & teacher notes

    This is a generic Fe-like atom (56 u, ionization 7.9 eV, excitation 2.5 eV), not an identified observed transition. A single LTE absorbing screen uses F=exp(−τ); opacity scales with 10^[M/H] and Saha–Boltzmann populations. T is set equal to Teff. Electron density ne=10¹⁹ × 10^(log g−4.44) × 5772/T m⁻³ is an explicit schematic pressure proxy, not a calibrated atmosphere.     Start here

    The continuum is a useful reference level beneath the forest of stellar spectral lines. Fraunhofer absorption lines reveal atomic and molecular interactions in the Sun’s atmosphere. A line can change when temperature, gravity, chemistry or the observing instrument changes.

    Go deeper · the mathematics

    Radiative transfer balances extinction and emission: dIν/ds = −ανIν + jν. Optical depth measures accumulated extinction. Effective temperature describes total emergent flux via F = σSB Teff⁴; it is not the temperature at every depth.

    Advanced · assumptions and scientific limits

    LTE uses local temperature and density relations for populations and source functions under appropriate assumptions. NLTE solves populations coupled to radiation and collisions. One-dimensional atmospheres, mean-3D structures, independent 1.5D columns and fully coupled 3D transfer have different assumptions. Microturbulence changes line opacity and saturation; it is not merely a final convolution. Thermal, rotational and instrumental broadening also have distinct effects.

    Worked example / activity

    Hold abundance fixed while comparing two schematic lines: an initially weak line responds differently to microturbulent broadening than a saturated line. A quantitative prediction requires a validated atmosphere, line data and transfer model; no such stellar grid is supplied here.

    Common misconception

    A deeper line does not necessarily mean more of an element. Temperature, ionization, blends, continuum placement and resolution can produce similar changes. An NLTE label alone does not establish applicability.

    Definitions: continuum · effective temperature · log g · metallicity · microturbulence · lte · nlte

    Model, sources & teacher notes

    Wλ = ∫[1−Fλ/Fc]dλ; 1 Å = 1000 mÅ. The center is a hypothetical 5000 Å (500 nm) line. Depth and width are independent synthetic teaching inputs. Gaussian full EW = depth × sigma × sqrt(2π); FWHM = 2 sqrt(2 ln 2) sigma and is not EW. A finite window can lose area. Continuum errors can add or subtract area, including a signed negative result.     Start here

    Start with calibrated spectra and verified source identity, wavelength scale and velocity frame. Correct contaminating telluric absorption where needed, estimate the continuum, then compare selected lines with a model. Equivalent widths and spectrum synthesis offer complementary diagnostics. A solar benchmark tests the chain before interpretation.

    Go deeper · the mathematics

    Equivalent width Wλ = ∫(1 − Fλ/Fc)dλ measures missing area relative to the continuum and has wavelength units. A(X) = log10(NX/NH) + 12. [X/H] = A(X)star − A(X)Sun on a compatible scale. [X/Fe] = [X/H] − [Fe/H].

    Advanced · assumptions and scientific limits

    VALD3 supplies atomic data with source provenance; it does not certify every line as suitable. Oscillator-strength grades, damping, blends, atmosphere choices and model applicability require review. Correlated gf or continuum errors do not shrink like independent noise. Propagate covariance and parameter sensitivity. IR measurements require holding-specific telluric validation before any claim of validated abundances.

    Worked example / activity

    Hypothetical values [X/H] = +0.20 dex and [Fe/H] = +0.10 dex give [X/Fe] = +0.10 dex. The X/H number ratio is 10⁰·² ≈ 1.58 times the reference solar ratio. These invented teaching inputs are not Codex measurements.

    Common misconception

    A line-list entry is not a quality grade. Averaging more lines cannot remove a shared systematic error; a solar agreement alone cannot validate compensating modeling errors.

    Start here

    Transits measure changes in stellar brightness when a planet crosses the stellar disk. Radial velocities track periodic motion of the star through shifting spectral lines. Stellar abundances describe the host’s photosphere; they help frame planet-formation questions alongside planetary observations and models.

    Go deeper · the mathematics

    For a small opaque planet and a uniform stellar disk, transit depth δ ≈ (Rp/Rstar)². Real analyses include limb darkening and system geometry. Radial-velocity amplitude constrains a mass–inclination combination in a single-planet Keplerian model, given stellar mass and orbit.

    Advanced · assumptions and scientific limits

    Planet formation redistributes and differentiates material. A photospheric [X/Fe] ratio is not a direct measurement of a planet’s bulk composition, atmosphere or biosignatures. Stellar activity, stellar parameters, orbital geometry and detection selection all affect interpretation.

    Worked example / activity

    A hypothetical 1% transit depth implies Rp/Rstar ≈ 0.10 under the uniform-disk approximation. It does not determine the planet’s mass, mineralogy or habitability. Compare this geometric inference with the chemistry inferred in the previous lesson.

    Common misconception

    An element associated with life in a host star is not evidence of life on its planets. A stellar C/O ratio does not uniquely specify a planet’s rocks or atmosphere.

    Start here

    A theoretical atomic calculation predicts properties from a model of the atom. A laboratory experiment measures a physical system. Evaluated databases may combine both. Always follow an oscillator strength back to its actual method and reference.

    Go deeper · the mathematics

    Line lists often report log10(gf), where g is the lower-state statistical weight and f the absorption oscillator strength. Errors in gf affect inferred abundance; the relationship depends on line strength and the model. Gauge agreement or convergence is diagnostic evidence, not independent experimental validation.

    Advanced · assumptions and scientific limits

    An ungraded line can be explored without calling its abundance scale trusted. Compare consistent transition identities, air/vacuum scales, fine-structure components, uncertainty definitions and independent literature. Agreement between engines sharing the same inputs is not an independent atomic-data check. Codex research predictions and unfinished validation gates are not established laboratory results.

    Worked example / activity

    Open the Atomic Laboratory roadmap and the Frontier atlas. For a selected entry, separate availability, implementation status, applicable physical domain and independent validation. Missing evidence stays unknown; membership in a literature list is not a grade.

    Common misconception

    A theoretical oscillator strength does not become an experimental measurement because it appears in an evaluated database. A successful software run does not supply scientific sign-off.

    Working lessons: light and color, prism/grating dispersion, resolution and Doppler shift; H/Na atomic transitions; a schematic stellar slab; EW and curve of growth; transit and stellar RV; atomic metadata; an interactive assessment. Scientific and accessibility review is pending.

    Future extensions: validated stellar-atmosphere grids, Planck continuum, explicit multi-line blends, rotational/macroturbulent broadening and fitted observations. The current stellar control panel is an explicitly schematic absorbing slab, not a grid prediction.

    Future research integrations: independently validated theoretical Atomic Laboratory candidates and advanced 3D NLTE exercises. Current metadata is pinned to cited canonical records; unknown and ungraded quantities remain explicit.

    Lessons, GUIs, classroom exercises and downloads remain free without subscriptions or registration. Illustrations are original code-drawn diagrams.
