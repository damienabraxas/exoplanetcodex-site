JWST Context — August 2026 Re-Observation

A candidate Saturn-mass planet around Alpha Cen A was detected by the JWST MIRI coronagraph. A follow-up observation is scheduled for August 2026 to confirm the detection. The Codex is measuring Alpha Cen A's stellar abundances in parallel to provide host-star chemistry context for any confirmed planet.

Stellar abundances from this pipeline will constrain the planet's likely bulk composition and volatile inventory — the same analysis being performed for 55 Cnc A, but with JWST time pressure driving urgency.
