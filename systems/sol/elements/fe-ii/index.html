Error-bar forest

How to read this forest. Each measured row is a separate abundance product for an instrument, holding, line pool and treatment; engines are never averaged together. Each bar is the uncertainty as Asplund et al. 2021 quote it: line-to-line scatter, the 3D-model term and the NLTE term (at least 0.03 dex). Atomic-data and observational terms are not in the bar, as in Asplund; they stay in the full error budget attached to the row. Green and gold regions mark the Asplund and Lodders literature values and uncertainties, not pass/fail gates. Grades describe the line pool’s provenance and selection, not the quality of an engine. Reddish-orange mean-3D / Frankenstein rows are experimental and not adopted. Yellow rows are Deep Grade: strong lines that saturate, kept but less certain than the other grades. Unmeasured model rows remain N/A.

Model labels

1D-LTE uses a one-dimensional atmosphere with atomic populations in local thermodynamic equilibrium. 1D-NLTE allows departures from that equilibrium; the named implementation supplies the correction or synthesis treatment.

Mean-3D / Frankenstein combines a spatially and temporally averaged 3D atmosphere with the Gerber synthesis route. Averaging removes the full 3D fluctuations and velocity field; this experimental hybrid is not a full 3D calculation or an adopted engine.

Synthesis fits the spectral line profile. See Methodology for the full model, line-selection and uncertainty procedures.

Solar iron context

Fe II traces singly ionized iron and is retained as an ionization and consistency diagnostic alongside Fe I, not an independent headline to average with it. The current resolved pool contains strong, saturation-sensitive lines and documented blend exclusions; the diagnostics below distinguish those limitations from model and holding differences.

Near-UV opacity — report and noted

Molecular opacity INCLUDED — OH A-X, NH, CH and CN violet, 6,223 VALD lines as Turbospectrum lists with the dominant isotopologue (RYA-1207). The band previously carried none: every molecular list iSpec ships begins at 400 nm. Measured PAIRED against a molecules-off run on the same code and pool, the lever is -0.047..-0.055 dex on Fe I and -0.276..-0.332 on Fe II, with chi2r median 81.8 -> 67.4 and control windows containing no molecular line bit-identical. RYA-1209 additionally curated out Fe I 3721.272 (systematic:K07 — gf attributed to a VALD3 reference that publishes no per-line uncertainty, no NIST accuracy class, so it never met the DEEPGRADED tier's lab-gf definition); the Fe I pool is 57 candidates and the budget clears at gf rung 3 (cited lab), 55/55 GF-LAB, RMS 0.0509 dex. RESIDUAL DEFICIT: the correction still UNDER-corrects — RYA-1204 measured synth/obs above 1 on 24 of 40 windows after it — so the band remains short of opacity by an amount not yet measured on this pool, and these values are a floor rather than a converged answer. sigma_syst is unchanged at 0.1122 because it is dominated by the 0.10 dex pseudo-continuum term, not by gf: the true continuum is never observed here. Not adopted for the headline. The headline is the per-band best-available product, selected from this feed by the publication generator rather than fixed as a constant: RYA-850 promoted the graded laboratory-gf pool to the PRIMARY reported value, and RYA-819 retired the earlier constructed anchor after showing its Magic-2013 correction wrong in both magnitude and shape. RYA-161: nothing calibrated to a target.

Frankenstein — experimental, pending the Bride

48 experimental products remain available in the Fe I product values and downloads.

RYA-1197 Part A: the Frankenstein mean-<3D> route is HELD FROM HEADLINE publication until the Bride validates it. It inflates A(Fe) by roughly +0.10 dex against the 1D siblings and exists on VIS/KP only, so it is not a comparable measurement of the same quantity. It stays in the feed deliberately: the LTE/NLTE mean-3D PAIR is the NLTE differential on ONE atmosphere (RYA-542), which is the diagnostic it is for. Not a headline value.

Saturation characterization

Committed proof census: 120 line/holding records; 100% have observed depth > 0.70. Median observed log10(EW[mÅ]/λ[Å]) = -1.252. In consistent Å units this is -4.252. These diagnostics characterize the audited saturated pool, not every newly added product.
