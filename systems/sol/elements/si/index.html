How this was measured

Every silicon value on this page comes from fitting synthetic spectra to the observed solar spectrum, line by line or band by band, with the silicon abundance as the free parameter. The synthesis includes the atomic lines and the molecular opacity in every window, so blended absorption is modelled rather than attributed to the line being measured. The continuum is placed locally around every line by comparing the observation with the synthesis on its own highest pixels, and ground-based spectra are telluric-corrected across their full range.

Each indicator is measured independently on each solar spectrum and reported separately; nothing is averaged across indicators or instruments. Where a 3D or NLTE correction is published for the lines, it is applied as its own product (1D-LTE, 1D-NLTE and 3D-NLTE are separate rows). Every value carries a full uncertainty budget: fit, line strengths, microturbulence, continuum, fit window, model atmosphere, telluric residual, and the spread between spectra, each measured by refitting with that one input changed. The headline at the top is the Asplund-grade product: a published line set, the most complete treatment, then the most lines.

Excluded lines

These lines are left out of the Sun's measurement. They stay in the line lists: a cooler or more evolved star may measure them well. Blended lines are not excluded -- the spectrum synthesis models the blend. 59 lines.

  • Abundance outlier: disagrees with the other lines well beyond its error, and no solar analysis in the literature uses it.
  • Not measurable: the line falls in a data gap or a saturated telluric band.
  • Saturated: on the flat part of the curve of growth, so the line barely responds to the abundance (reduced width log W/λ above -4.9).

Solar silicon context

Silicon is traced by Si I lines from the blue to the H band and by the single Si II 6371 Å line. The optical lines are Asplund et al. 2021's set (Amarsi & Asplund 2017) with the gf the authors used; the infrared lines are the published sets of Deshmukh et al. 2022 and Elgueta et al. 2026 on Pehlivan Rhodin et al. 2024 oscillator strengths. Each set, spectrum and treatment remains a separate product.
