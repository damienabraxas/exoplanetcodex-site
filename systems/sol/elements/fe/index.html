Error-bar forest

How to read this forest. Each measured row is a separate abundance product for an instrument, holding, line pool and treatment; engines are never averaged together. Each bar is the uncertainty as Asplund et al. 2021 quote it: line-to-line scatter, the 3D-model term and the NLTE term (at least 0.03 dex). Atomic-data and observational terms are not in the bar, as in Asplund; they stay in the full error budget attached to the row. Green and gold regions mark the Asplund and Lodders literature values and uncertainties, not pass/fail gates. Grades describe the line pool’s provenance and selection, not the quality of an engine. Reddish-orange mean-3D / Frankenstein rows are experimental and not adopted. Unmeasured model rows remain N/A.

Grades and model labels
  • Reference Grade. An externally published reference line pool, using that reference’s line selection and oscillator strengths; the current Fe reference products use AGSS21. This is a provenance category, not a promise that every line has an individual primary-laboratory uncertainty.
  • Codex Grade. The vetted Codex pool of primary-laboratory oscillator strengths, restricted to lines at or below the adopted feature-depth gate.
  • Deep Grade. The corresponding laboratory-data pool above that depth gate. These stronger lines extend coverage but require particular care with saturation and blends.

1D-LTE uses a one-dimensional atmosphere with atomic populations in local thermodynamic equilibrium. 1D-NLTE allows departures from that equilibrium; the named implementation supplies the correction or synthesis treatment.

3D-NLTE (Amarsi) includes three-dimensional atmospheric structure and non-LTE line formation; these products apply the published Amarsi correction grid to a 1D baseline.

Mean-3D / Frankenstein combines a spatially and temporally averaged 3D atmosphere with the Gerber synthesis route. Averaging removes the full 3D fluctuations and velocity field; this experimental hybrid is not a full 3D calculation or an adopted engine.

Synthesis fits the spectral line profile. EW infers abundance from a line’s integrated absorption (equivalent width). See Methodology for the full model, line-selection and uncertainty procedures.

Solar iron context

Fe I supplies the bulk of the neutral-iron line statistics used for the Solar abundance reference. Separate instruments, line pools and treatments test the stability of that reference; their differences remain visible.

Near-UV opacity — report and noted

Molecular opacity INCLUDED — OH A-X, NH, CH and CN violet, 6,223 VALD lines as Turbospectrum lists with the dominant isotopologue (RYA-1207). The band previously carried none: every molecular list iSpec ships begins at 400 nm. Measured PAIRED against a molecules-off run on the same code and pool, the lever is -0.047..-0.055 dex on Fe I and -0.276..-0.332 on Fe II, with chi2r median 81.8 -> 67.4 and control windows containing no molecular line bit-identical. RYA-1209 additionally curated out Fe I 3721.272 (systematic:K07 — gf attributed to a VALD3 reference that publishes no per-line uncertainty, no NIST accuracy class, so it never met the DEEPGRADED tier's lab-gf definition); the Fe I pool is 57 candidates and the budget clears at gf rung 3 (cited lab), 55/55 GF-LAB, RMS 0.0509 dex. RESIDUAL DEFICIT: the correction still UNDER-corrects — RYA-1204 measured synth/obs above 1 on 24 of 40 windows after it — so the band remains short of opacity by an amount not yet measured on this pool, and these values are a floor rather than a converged answer. sigma_syst is unchanged at 0.1122 because it is dominated by the 0.10 dex pseudo-continuum term, not by gf: the true continuum is never observed here. Not adopted for the headline. The headline is the per-band best-available product, selected from this feed by the publication generator rather than fixed as a constant: RYA-850 promoted the graded laboratory-gf pool to the PRIMARY reported value, and RYA-819 retired the earlier constructed anchor after showing its Magic-2013 correction wrong in both magnitude and shape.
