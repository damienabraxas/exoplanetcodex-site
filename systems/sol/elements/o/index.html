How this was measured

Every oxygen value on this page comes from fitting synthetic spectra to the observed solar spectrum, line by line or band by band, with the oxygen abundance as the free parameter. The synthesis includes the atomic lines and the molecular bands (CN, CH, C₂, OH and others) in every window, so blended absorption is modelled rather than attributed to the line being measured. The continuum is placed locally around every line by comparing the observation with the synthesis on its own highest pixels, and ground-based spectra are telluric-corrected across their full range.

Each indicator is measured independently on each solar spectrum and reported separately; nothing is averaged across indicators or instruments. Where a 3D or NLTE correction is published for the lines, it is applied as its own product (1D-LTE, 1D-NLTE and 3D-NLTE are separate rows). Every value carries a full uncertainty budget: fit, line strengths, microturbulence, continuum, fit window, model atmosphere, telluric residual, blends and coupling to the other C/N/O abundances, and the spread between spectra, each measured by refitting with that one input changed. The headline at the top is the Asplund-grade product: a published line set, the most complete treatment, then the most lines.

Solar oxygen context

Oxygen is traced by forbidden [O I], permitted O I and molecular OH indicators where available. Blends, departures from LTE and atmospheric structure affect these families differently, so their results retain separate identities.
