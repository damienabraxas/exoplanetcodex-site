How to read this forest. Each row is one independent product, preserving its band, holding, engine, indicator and line set. Engines and indicator families are never averaged. Each bar is the total uncertainty -- the statistical and systematic terms combined in quadrature, as Asplund et al. 2021 and Lodders et al. 2025 quote it. The full error budget remains attached to the row. Grades describe the supplied line-pool provenance, not engine quality; evaluated theoretical CNO oscillator strengths are not relabelled as laboratory evidence. Quarantined, failed and unconstrained fits have no abundance mark. Experimental products remain explicitly not adopted. Literature references provide context, never tuning targets.

How this was measured

Every magnesium value on this page comes from fitting synthetic spectra to the observed solar spectrum, line by line or band by band, with the magnesium abundance as the free parameter. The synthesis includes the atomic lines and the molecular opacity in every window, so blended absorption is modelled rather than attributed to the line being measured. The continuum is placed locally around every line by comparing the observation with the synthesis on its own highest pixels, and ground-based spectra are telluric-corrected across their full range.

Each indicator is measured independently on each solar spectrum and reported separately; nothing is averaged across indicators or instruments. Where a 3D or NLTE correction is published for the lines, it is applied as its own product (1D-LTE, 1D-NLTE and 3D-NLTE are separate rows). Every value carries a full uncertainty budget: fit, line strengths, microturbulence, continuum, fit window, model atmosphere, telluric residual, and the spread between spectra, each measured by refitting with that one input changed. The headline at the top is the Asplund-grade product: a published line set, the most complete treatment, then the most lines.

Solar magnesium context

Magnesium is measured separately from neutral Mg I and ionized Mg II. Asplund et al. 2021 use eight Mg I and six Mg II features, average the lines within each ion, then weight the two ion means by their total uncertainties. Both ion results are required for the combined abundance.
