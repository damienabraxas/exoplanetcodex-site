Nitrogen Appendix

N · products, publication readiness and reproducible evidence

Solar nitrogen: 7.883 ± 0.182

Asplund, Amarsi & Grevesse 2021: 7.83 ± 0.07  ·  this measurement +0.053 dex

The solar N I lines are weak (0.3–1% deep) and carry CN absorption inside their own profiles, so the result depends on molecular opacity and on continuum placement at the 0.1% level. Both are now modelled: CN is synthesised with the line, and the continuum is placed locally around every line. For comparison, Asplund et al. 2021 give 7.77 from N I after empirically rescaling the CN blends, and Lodders, Bergemann & Palme 2025 adopt 7.94 ± 0.11 from the two least-blended lines. The error bar carries the full uncertainty budget, including CN blending and telluric residuals.

How this was measured

Every nitrogen value on this page comes from fitting synthetic spectra to the observed solar spectrum, line by line or band by band, with the nitrogen abundance as the free parameter. The synthesis includes the atomic lines and the molecular bands (CN, CH, C₂, OH and others) in every window, so blended absorption is modelled rather than attributed to the line being measured. The continuum is placed locally around every line by comparing the observation with the synthesis on its own highest pixels, and ground-based spectra are telluric-corrected across their full range.

Each indicator is measured independently on each solar spectrum and reported separately; nothing is averaged across indicators or instruments. Where a 3D or NLTE correction is published for the lines, it is applied as its own product (1D-LTE, 1D-NLTE and 3D-NLTE are separate rows). Every value carries a full uncertainty budget: fit, line strengths, microturbulence, continuum, fit window, model atmosphere, telluric residual, blends and coupling to the other C/N/O abundances, and the spread between spectra, each measured by refitting with that one input changed. The headline at the top is the Asplund-grade product: a published line set, the most complete treatment, then the most lines.

Solar nitrogen context

The nitrogen strategy uses red-optical N I as the primary atomic route, with NH providing an independent molecular check and CN a carbon-dependent cross-check. An unresolved or unconstrained fit supplies no adopted Solar nitrogen abundance.
