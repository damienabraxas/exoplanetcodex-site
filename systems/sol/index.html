Section 02

Our Findings

The Sun is not a target — it is the ruler. Every abundance in the Codex is expressed as [X/H] = A(X)★ − A(X)☉, which means getting the solar reference right is the single most important calibration decision in the pipeline. We use 10 direct HARPS S1D exposures (ESO 1102.D-0954(A), PI: Dumusque) — a direct solar feed, not asteroid-reflected sunlight — with SNR 306–309 per exposure.

Problem Lines — Active Development

[O I] 6300.304 Å — Ni I Blend

The forbidden oxygen line at 6300.304 Å is blended with Ni I at 6300.336 Å (Δλ = 0.032 Å). At HARPS resolution (R~115,000) they are marginally resolved but the Ni contribution must be computed from the measured Ni abundance and subtracted analytically. Until Ni is cleanly measured in the solar calibration run, this blend correction propagates uncertainty into C/O. Currently: O I has no usable lines returned by the pipeline.

Li I 6707.76 Å — CN Blend

The lithium doublet at 6707.76 + 6707.91 Å is contaminated by CN molecular lines in cool, metal-rich stars. In the Sun this is a minor issue, but in 55 Cancri A ([Fe/H] = +0.32) CN blending becomes significant. The pipeline currently flags all Li I measurements in the solar run as nonlinear-excluded. Accurate Li requires an updated CN linelist and deblending step — planned for Milestone B.
