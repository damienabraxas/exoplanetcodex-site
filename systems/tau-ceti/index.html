Science Context

Tau Ceti is a metal-poor counterpoint to 55 Cancri A. At [Fe/H] ≈ −0.50, it sits at the low-metallicity end of the FGK target list — roughly 3× less iron than the Sun and 6× less than 55 Cnc A. This makes it a valuable anchor point for tracking how CHNOPS ratios, C/O, and Mg/Si vary with stellar metallicity.

4–5 candidate planets have been identified from radial velocity studies, with planets e and f falling near the habitable zone. Whether the system's low metallicity translates to depleted rocky planet building blocks — or more volatile-rich planets — is a key science question this entry will address.

Section 03

Planets

COMING SOON — Planet parameter table will appear here when this entry activates.

4–5 candidate planets identified via radial velocity (Feng et al. 2017). Planets e and f are candidates for the habitable zone. All are super-Earth to mini-Neptune class. Planet candidates remain unconfirmed by transit or direct imaging as of May 2026.
